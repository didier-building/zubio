# Chatwoot User Personas

## 1. Support Agent Personas

### 👨‍💼 Alex - Entry-Level Support Agent
- **Age**: 22-28
- **Technical Skill**: Moderate
- **Experience**: 0-2 years in customer support
- **Goals**: 
  - Handle customer queries efficiently
  - Meet response time targets
  - Learn company products
- **Pain Points**:
  - Gets overwhelmed by complex queries
  - Needs frequent reference to knowledge base
  - Sometimes struggles with multiple conversations
- **How They Use Chatwoot**:
  - Primarily responds to straightforward queries
  - Uses canned responses extensively
  - Relies on conversation history to understand context
  - Appreciates clear routing of conversations
  - Uses the knowledge base integration often

### 👩‍💼 Maya - Senior Support Specialist
- **Age**: 28-40
- **Technical Skill**: High
- **Experience**: 3-10 years in customer support
- **Goals**:
  - Resolve complex customer issues
  - Maintain high customer satisfaction
  - Coach junior agents
- **Pain Points**:
  - Frustrated by limited tools for complex issue resolution
  - Needs deeper analytics to improve processes
  - Wants better handoff between teams
- **How They Use Chatwoot**:
  - Handles escalated conversations
  - Creates and refines canned responses
  - Uses advanced filters to prioritize workload
  - Adds detailed notes for team context
  - Customizes personal dashboard

## 2. Admin Personas

### 👨‍💼 Ray - Customer Support Manager
- **Age**: 30-45
- **Technical Skill**: Moderate to High
- **Experience**: 5+ years in support management
- **Goals**:
  - Monitor team performance
  - Optimize support processes
  - Maintain service quality
- **Pain Points**:
  - Needs clear visibility into team productivity
  - Struggles to balance workloads across team
  - Wants to identify bottlenecks in support workflow
- **How They Use Chatwoot**:
  - Monitors conversation analytics
  - Reviews agent performance metrics
  - Sets up team structures and routing rules
  - Creates reports for higher management
  - Configures automation rules

### 👩‍💼 Priya - Startup Founder/Solo Entrepreneur
- **Age**: 25-40
- **Technical Skill**: Varies (Often tech-savvy)
- **Experience**: Expert in own product, varied in support
- **Goals**:
  - Provide excellent support with limited resources
  - Scale support operations with business growth
  - Understand customer needs directly
- **Pain Points**:
  - Limited time to handle support alongside other duties
  - Needs to maximize efficiency
  - Wants simple setup without technical complexity
- **How They Use Chatwoot**:
  - Handles most conversations directly
  - Sets up automation for common questions
  - Uses mobile app for on-the-go support
  - Values integrated channels to maintain single inbox
  - Relies on analytics to plan future hires

## 3. Customer Personas

### 👨‍💻 Sam - Technical Customer
- **Age**: 20-45
- **Technical Skill**: High
- **Patience Level**: Low to Moderate
- **Goals**:
  - Get quick, accurate answers
  - Solve technical problems
  - Minimize back-and-forth
- **Pain Points**:
  - Dislikes repeating technical details
  - Frustrated by non-technical agents
  - Expects fast responses
- **How They Interact With Chatwoot**:
  - Uses direct, technical language
  - Prefers chat over other channels
  - May share screenshots/code snippets
  - Values persistent conversation history

### 👵 Barbara - Non-Technical Customer
- **Age**: 40-70+
- **Technical Skill**: Low
- **Patience Level**: Varies
- **Goals**:
  - Get help with basic product usage
  - Clear explanation in non-technical terms
  - Feel secure and supported
- **Pain Points**:
  - Confused by technical jargon
  - May need step-by-step guidance
  - Sometimes anxious about digital interactions
- **How They Interact With Chatwoot**:
  - Prefers simple, clear communication
  - May take longer to respond
  - Values personalized, patient support
  - Might prefer phone or email to chat

### 👨‍💼 Jordan - Business Customer
- **Age**: 30-55
- **Technical Skill**: Moderate
- **Patience Level**: Low
- **Goals**:
  - Quick resolution of business-critical issues
  - Minimal impact on operations
  - Clear communication about timelines
- **Pain Points**:
  - Any delay costs money
  - Needs to report status to stakeholders
  - Expects priority treatment
- **How They Interact With Chatwoot**:
  - Direct and results-focused communication
  - May escalate quickly if unsatisfied
  - Values transparency about issue status
  - Expects follow-up communication

## 4. Developer/Integrator Persona

### 👨‍💻 Dev - System Integrator
- **Age**: 25-40
- **Technical Skill**: Very High
- **Experience**: 3+ years in development
- **Goals**:
  - Integrate Chatwoot into existing systems
  - Customize for specific business needs
  - Extend functionality through API
- **Pain Points**:
  - Needs clear, complete documentation
  - Frustrated by API limitations
  - Requires stable endpoints and clear versioning
- **How They Use Chatwoot**:
  - Works primarily with API
  - May contribute to open source codebase
  - Creates custom integrations
  - Uses webhooks for event-driven workflows
  - Needs sandbox environment for testing
